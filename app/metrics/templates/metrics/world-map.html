Welcome to the Training Metrics Database!

The Training Metrics Database is an effort to streamline data collection, storage, and visualisation for the Quality and Impact of the ELIXIR Training Platform services.

The TMD contains data describing the audience demographic being reached by ELIXIR training events in addition to assessing the quality of ELIXIR training events from the users perspective. It also contains data evaluating the longer term impact that ELIXIR training events have had on the work of training participants. Most of the data presented here is collected in shape of post workshop feedback surveys (immediately after the training or about half a year after the training) and registration data.

Please note that data entry may be delayed and is under the responsibility of the ELIXIR Nodes training coordinator. This also means that the TMD showing 0 events for a node, does not mean that they do not do training.

Further information (calendar, training material) about the trainings can be found on ELIXIR TeSS.
